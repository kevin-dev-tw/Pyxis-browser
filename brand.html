Brand guidelines

PYXIS.

A local-first browser for Windows — no account, no sync, no telemetry. Quiet surfaces, one warm signal.

01 — Logo

The mark is the ember flame in a rounded plate. Use it at 16–96px; below 16px it loses legibility. Never stretch, recolor, add shadows or place it on clashing imagery.

02 — Color

Neutral surfaces first, then one warm accent. Ember is signal only — CTAs, active states, focus, brand marks. No second accent, ever. The product ships both palettes; use them as pairs.

03 — Typography

Interface voice is quiet sans; metadata speaks mono. Uppercase micro-labels with wide tracking are the signature.

Display — Segoe UI Variable, tight tracking
Your browser. Your disk.
Mono — JetBrains Mono, +0.18em tracking, uppercase
CTRL+SHIFT+Z · LOCAL-FIRST · 0 TELEMETRY

04 — Layout

Flat surfaces, hairline borders, no shadows — elevation is shown by the border, not the blur. Corners use a three-step radius system: 8px controls, 12px panels, pill for capsules. A sparse 28px dot grid may texture the start page only.

DoHairline borders · 8/12/pill radius · mono micro-labels · single accent · flat, full-bleed sections
Don'tDrop shadows · gradients · second accent · glass blur · serif or Inter · emojis

05 — Voice

Cold, precise, honest. State what the software does and what it doesn't — including its edges. No marketing verbs (elevate, unleash, seamless), no emojis, no hype.

Sounds like"0 telemetry." · "Your data never leaves the disk." · "Unsigned build — right-click → unblock."
Never"The next-gen browsing experience" · "Elevate your workflow" · emoji bullets
