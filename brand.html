PYXIS // BRAND GUIDELINES

The blueprint browser.

A local-first browser for Windows — no account, no sync, no telemetry.

01 — Logo

The mark is the ember flame in a square plate. Use it at 16–96px; below 16px it loses legibility. Never stretch, recolor, add shadows or place it on clashing imagery.

02 — Color

One warm accent over cool neutral surfaces. Ember is signal only — warnings, active states, focus rings, brand marks. Saturation stays under 80%; no second accent ever.

03 — Typography

Interface voice is quiet sans; metadata speaks mono. Uppercase micro-labels with wide tracking are the signature.

Display — Segoe UI Variable, −2 to −3.5px tracking
Browse without being watched.
Mono — JetBrains Mono, +0.18em tracking, uppercase
CTRL+SHIFT+Z · EASYLIST ENGINE · 0 TELEMETRY

04 — Layout

Structure is drawn, not painted: hairline rule grid (28px), square corners (0 radius), 1px Rule Hairline borders instead of shadows. Square is the brand.

DOHairline grids · square corners · mono micro-labels · single accent · flat elevation
DON'TRounded corners · drop shadows · gradients · second accent · serif or Inter · emojis

05 — Voice

Cold, precise, honest. State what the software does and what it doesn't — including its edges. No marketing verbs (elevate, unleash, seamless), no emojis, no hype.

SOUNDS LIKE"0 telemetry." · "Your data never leaves the disk." · "Known edges: Google search still sees queries."
NEVER"The next-gen browsing experience" · "Elevate your workflow" · emoji bullets
